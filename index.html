FAQ

Common questions

Do I need prior Excel experience?
The Associate programme assumes basic familiarity with Excel. The Expert programme is designed for those who already hold the Associate credential or have equivalent proficiency.
How long does each programme take?
Both the Associate and Expert programmes require approximately 40 hours of commitment, typically completed over five days.
What is the Microsoft MOS exam format?
MOS exams are performance-based tests delivered through Certiport testing centres. You complete real tasks inside Microsoft Excel within a set time — typically 50 minutes.
What happens if I do not pass the MOS Exam on my first attempt?
If you do not pass the MOS exam on your first attempt, you are welcome to enrol in our class one more time at no additional cost.
Is the content suitable for Excel on Mac?
The MOS Excel 2019 exam is administered on Windows. We recommend completing the course using Excel 2019 or Microsoft 365 on a Windows device to ensure full feature parity.
Will I receive a certificate after completion?
Yes — you receive a BlueBird completion certificate upon finishing the course. The official Microsoft credential is awarded by Certiport after passing the proctored exam.
